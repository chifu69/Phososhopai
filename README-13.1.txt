PHOTO IA 13.1 — Photo Critic + Regional Intelligence

Base: PHOTO IA 13.0 Professional Adaptive Workspace supplied by the user.
The visual interface and Adaptive Workspace layout were preserved.

Implemented:
- Smart analysis reads the original photograph and does not stack enhancements.
- Photo Critic evaluates exposure, skin texture, noise, clipping and focus before editing.
- Portrait protection reduces facial clarity and prevents blemishes, pores and wrinkles from becoming more visible.
- Conversational local commands for a dark/bright face, tired appearance, skin, background and identification photos.
- Regional editing for face, skin and background while preserving the rest of the image.
- Selection profiles: full person, bust/ID, face and skin.
- Restore background without discarding edited layers.
- Mask refinement for softer edges and fewer holes/halos.
- PWA version and caches updated to 13.1.

Not changed:
- Adaptive Workspace UI layout.
- Alienware connection, HTTPS, token, ports, certificates or ComfyUI workflow.
