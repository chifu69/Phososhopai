PHOTO IA 15.7 — Portrait / ID Full-Image Editing

- Busto / identificación no longer depends on a client-side bust mask.
- The full original photograph is sent to the Alienware as context.
- Portrait/ID mode uses structured preservation instructions: identity, facial geometry, hair, body proportions, pose, clothing, background, perspective and composition.
- Focus is face + head + neck + shoulders, with low edit strength.
- Only photographic quality should change: exposure, white balance, natural skin tone, noise and moderate detail.
- No blue bust selection overlay is created when entering Portrait/ID mode.
- Existing masks remain available for face, skin, hair, clothing, person and object-by-touch workflows where masks are useful.
- Keeps smart_edit profile compatibility from 15.5.
